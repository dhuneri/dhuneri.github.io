02B — The crop

A spice rooted
in Nepal’s hills.

Black cardamom, locally known as alaichi (अलैंची) in Nepal, is an aromatic spice derived from the dried fruit capsules of Amomum subulatum.

Explore the 4 Origin Terroirs Click district:
1,800m – 2,200m

Taplejung Terroir: Glacial air beneath Mt. Kanchenjunga yields dense, resinous pods with penetrating camphor depth and wild herbal strength.

Behind every pod lies more than its distinctive flavour; it reflects generations of local knowledge, skilled hands, and careful craftsmanship, from tending the perennial clumps under mountain alder shade to selective harvesting and fire-drying the capsules.

“From the heart of Nepal’s hills to the heart of your kitchen.”
