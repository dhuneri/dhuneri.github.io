Large cardamom (Amomum subulatum) grows in humid shade across Nepal’s hills. Its journey from plant to pouch brings together the land, the hands that harvest it, and the care taken after harvest.

Explore each stage to see how the crop moves from the field through curing, grading and packing — and onward to your kitchen.

Stage 01 · Growing conditions

The plant.

Large cardamom (Amomum subulatum) grows 1.5–3.0 metres tall in humid shade. In the eastern hills, it grows at 765–1,675 metres, with temperatures of 4–20°C and 2,000–2,500 mm of rain.

Stage 03 · Where flavour is made

The cure — where flavour is made.

Roughly 48–72 hours over a low wood-fire smoulder in the traditional bhatti.

Direct smoke contact gives black cardamom its unmistakable smoky, camphor-rich character. This is the stage where time, heat and careful tending meet.

48–72hours · approximate curing time in the process brief

Stage 05 · Packing & batch code

A pouch with a beginning.

Pods are sealed in aroma-barrier pouches. Each pouch is coded to its farm, linking the pack back to the place where its journey began.
