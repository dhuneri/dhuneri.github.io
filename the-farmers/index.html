03 / INGREDIENT & SENSORY ARCHITECTURE

Inside the pod.
Smoke, mist and resin.

Wild-harvested Amomum subulatum contains delicate cineoles and terpenes destroyed by industrial machine drying. Over 50 hours of seasoned alder fire curing, whole pods lock in their vital character.

01

Mountain Camphor

A crisp, clearing top-note derived from 1,8-cineole (42.8% of volatiles). Cuts through rich fats in braises, broths, and spiced curries.

02

Alder Woodsmoke

Sweet, lingering guaiacol phenols infused over 50 hours of seasoned Utis hardwood embers. Deep hearth sweetness without acrid soot.

03

Resinous Warmth

2.85% essential oil yield—nearly double industrial bulk. 40 to 50 resin-packed seeds in every 22–26 mm First Cut capsule.

Three Kitchen Rituals

CHAI RITUAL

Kathmandu Fire Chai

Crack 2 pods into strong black tea, buffalo milk, and bruised ginger. The alder wood-smoke note rounds the sweetness into velvet.

HIMALAYAN FLAME

Smoked Momo Achar

Char 1 whole pod directly over flame with sun-dried Akabare chilies and ripe tomatoes. Blend with roasted sesame for premier dumplings.

AROMATIC BROTH

Kechi Pulao Pot

Submerge 1 whole pod into basmati rice broth. As the grains steam, it releases deep cedar aromatics. Remove before plating.
